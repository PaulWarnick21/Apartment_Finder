# Search preferences at the November 2026 pause

This describes the previous Vancouver search. The compact desk stores the applied settings
and scoring parameters. Confirm the neighbourhoods, budget and lease needs before live work
resumes. Revalidate older apartments before considering them for the active search.

## Intake and location

- Target whole one- or two-bedroom apartments on the downtown Vancouver peninsula with
  advertised monthly rent from $1,500 to $3,500 inclusive. Prioritize the West End,
  Stanley Park, Coal Harbour and convenient SkyTrain access to the airport. Include
  English Bay, Davie Village, Yaletown, Gastown and City Gate near Science World.
- Exclude confirmed locations outside the downtown boundary in the previous intake scope.
  Keep uncertain matches for full address verification. That review uses the actual
  street address, municipality and neighbourhood. Website filters and proximity claims
  cannot establish the address or the boundary result.
- Require private laundry equipment inside the unit. Shared laundry alone fails this
  requirement. Flag conflicting or missing details for confirmation. Quick ranking
  favors advertised in-unit laundry and unit parking. Lower the work priority of ads
  posted more than three months ago while retaining their eligibility for review.

## Review and scoring

- Value meaningful rent savings. Show parking and other recurring charges separately,
  including which amounts are unknown. An extra bedroom is more valuable at a suitable
  price. A verified den or dedicated office can make a one-bedroom more useful.
- Prefer mostly unobstructed mountains, ocean or distant forest visible from the actual
  unit. Building-dominated glimpses, street trees, amenity views and representative images
  cannot verify a strong outlook. Check every accessible gallery image in full review.
  Keep unknown views uncertain and apply at most one supported poor-view deduction of
  up to one point. Higher floors and supported north-facing outlooks are preferences.
- Distinguish included parking, paid parking, unavailable parking and unknown status.
  Included parking is strongly preferred. Missing information alone does not lower the score.
- Favor a modern interior, usable layout, open-plan living space and a confirmed gas
  cooktop when supported. Flag uncertain features. Treat furnishing as a convenience
  whose value depends on price and lease length. Record actual terms and renewal options.
- Record internet provider, connection, speeds and price when stated. Confirm service
  before signing. Generic claims leave reliability and performance unverified.
